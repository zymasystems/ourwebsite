01

The Brief

Grace Mission Church International is a growing church with an active ministry calendar. Its digital life, however, was spread across separate places — a website, social and video channels, and giving and communication tools that each lived on their own.

The brief for the GMC App is simple to state and demanding to build: connect members to their church from one place. Instead of chasing links across platforms, a member should be able to open a single app and find ministry teaching, read the Bible, give securely, and keep up with what is happening across every ministry.

🎯

One app, one login, every part of the church's digital experience — built for members who are on their phones, not at a desktop.

One home for the church's digital experience
Ministry content accessible on any device
Giving that is simple and secure
Tools for every ministry, not just the main service
02

Core Features

The feature set covers the full member journey — from creating an account and following ministry content, through to giving and the day-to-day work of running ministries.

🔐

Member Login & Sign-up

A member account that personalises the app and keeps each person's activity in one profile.

📺

Ministry Content

YouTube ministry content surfaced inside the app, so teaching is never more than a tap away.

📖

Bible Reading

Built-in Bible reading so members can study the Word without leaving the app.

💳

Secure Giving

A protected giving flow that makes tithes and offerings straightforward for every member.

🗂️

Ministry Management

Tools to manage every ministry across the church — not only the main congregation.
